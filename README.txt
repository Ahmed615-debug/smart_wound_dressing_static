SMART WOUND DRESSING — STATIC DEPLOYMENT

This version does not require Node.js, a server, or a backend.
All demo accounts, settings, alerts, and history are saved in browser LocalStorage on the current device/browser only. They do not sync between an iPad and laptop. Do not enter real patient data or reuse a real password. All sensor values are simulated and are not for medical diagnosis or treatment.

FAST HOSTING: Open https://app.netlify.com/drop on your laptop, sign in if prompted, then drag the entire smart_wound_dressing_static_site folder (or its contents) into the drop area. Netlify will publish a URL. The hosting provider may require an account and may change its interface.
